# AI-Powered Smart Eyewear: Bridging Human-AI Interaction Through AR Glasses & Multimodal AI

## 💡 The Opportunity

Wearable AI is transforming the way we interact with digital information. However, current smart eyewear lacks true context-awareness—they can process multimodal inputs (vision, audio, text) but fail to understand the user’s attention and intent. Inspired by recent breakthroughs like GPT-4o, our venture integrates eye-tracking and multimodal AI into next-gen AR glasses (RayNeo X2).

Our goal is to enable contextualized AI interactions, where the device intelligently understands what the user is focusing on, enhancing productivity, accessibility, and real-world applications.

💪 With the support of Dr. Jiankang Deng (Honorary Lecturer at Imperial), including access to cutting-edge hardware (RayNeo X2, RTX 5090 GPU) and research insights, we have a unique advantage to develop a proof-of-concept system that aligns with real-world commercial AI applications.

---

## 🎯 Our Vision

We aim to develop a **multimodal, context-aware AI assistant** for AR glasses that leverages:

- ✅ **Gaze Tracking** – AI understands user attention to deliver relevant real-time insights.
- ✅ **Multimodal AI** – Combining vision, speech, and contextual LLMs for intelligent interactions.
- ✅ **Wearable UX** – Optimizing hands-free AI experiences for accessibility, productivity & beyond.

### Potential application areas include:

- 👨‍💻 **Productivity** – AI-enhanced note-taking, real-time information lookup.
- 📚 **Education & Training** – Interactive learning with contextual insights.
- 🌍 **Accessibility** – AI-powered assistive technology for the visually impaired.
- 🛍️ **Retail & Marketing** – Augmented product recommendations.
- 🏥 **Healthcare** – Medical assistance and real-time diagnostics.

## 🔬 What We Will Build (AI Venture Focused)

Our goal is to develop a functional AI prototype and validate its commercial potential. This aligns with the AI Venture course by exploring real-world AI applications, product-market fit, and startup methodologies.

- ✅ **Phase 1: Market Exploration & Business Model**
  - Identify high-value application scenarios (Education? Productivity? Accessibility?)
  - Conduct customer validation and business modeling to shape the go-to-market strategy.

- ✅ **Phase 2: AI-Enhanced Prototype**
  - Deploy some well-established multi-modality models and develop a mobile-server web prototype application.
  - Implement a multimodal AI assistant on RayNeo X2 AR glasses.

- ✅ **Phase 3: Final Demo & Pitch**
  - Build a compelling product demo.
  - Develop a business case for commercial viability.
